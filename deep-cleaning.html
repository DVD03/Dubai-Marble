Service Overview

Deep Marble Cleaning Services

تنظيف عميق للرخام والجرانيت

Porous natural stones absorb moisture, dust, oil, and detergent residue over time. Our deep cleaning treatment uses specialized rotary machinery and imported pH-neutral detergents to lift embedded contaminants safely without harming delicate calcite crystals.

Key Benefits of Our Deep Marble Cleaning Services

  • Lifts deep ground-in soil and grease
  • Restores bright, clean grout lines
  • Protects against bacterial and mold buildup
  • Safe for children, pets, and indoor air quality
  • Ideal for annual or post-tenancy rejuvenation

Our 4-Step Professional Process

01. Free Inspection

We assess stone type, wear level, scratch depth, and provide a clear quotation.

02. Preparation

Walls, baseboards, and surrounding furniture are masked and protected.

03. Restoration

Multi-stage diamond treatment, crack filling, and high-gloss crystallization.

04. Sealing & Handover

Application of nano-penetrating sealer and final quality inspection.
