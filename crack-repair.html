Service Overview

Crack Filling & Joint Repair

تعبئة وإصلاح تشققات وفواصل الرخام

Settling buildings, heavy impacts, or thermal expansion can cause cracks, chips, and failed grout joints. We repair damaged marble using premium Italian resins mixed on-site to match the exact tone and vein structure of your stone.

Key Benefits of Our Crack Filling & Joint Repair

  • Seamless color-matched epoxy & polyester resins
  • Reinforces structural integrity and stops crack spread
  • Polished flush so repairs are virtually undetectable
  • Complete regrouting of deteriorated tile seams
  • Cost-effective alternative to stone replacement

Our 4-Step Professional Process

01. Free Inspection

We assess stone type, wear level, scratch depth, and provide a clear quotation.

02. Preparation

Walls, baseboards, and surrounding furniture are masked and protected.

03. Restoration

Multi-stage diamond treatment, crack filling, and high-gloss crystallization.

04. Sealing & Handover

Application of nano-penetrating sealer and final quality inspection.
