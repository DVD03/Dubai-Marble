Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Marble Polishing & Restoration in Dubai

How much does marble polishing cost in Dubai?
Marble restoration quotation depends on stone type, total area, and surface condition. We provide a 100% free on-site inspection across all Dubai communities followed by a fixed written quote with no hidden charges.
How long does the marble polishing process take?
A standard 100–150 sqm residential apartment usually takes 1 to 2 days. Larger villas or commercial hotel lobbies may require 2 to 4 days depending on the severity of damage and lippage.
Is there messy dust generated during the polishing?
No. We utilize advanced wet diamond polishing machinery. Water lubricates the diamond pads, trapping all stone dust into a slurry that is continuously vacuumed up with industrial wet vacuums.
Can you remove deep stains like wine, coffee, or acid etch marks?
Yes! We apply chemical poultice treatments designed specifically for marble that draw out organic and acidic stains from within the stone's pores without destroying its natural calcite structure.
How often should marble floors be polished in Dubai?
For residential homes, a professional crystallization every 12 to 18 months maintains a high gloss. For commercial venues and hotels with heavy foot traffic, we recommend maintenance every 3 to 6 months.
Do you offer a free inspection before quoting?
Yes, our senior technician will visit your location anywhere in Dubai free of charge, measure the area, test the stone, and give you an exact, transparent quote.
